About Blue Rock Manor

Neighborhood information and Civic Association details for Blue Rock Manor residents.

Neighborhood Information

Blue Rock Manor is a residential neighborhood in North Wilmington consisting of 201 homes in the Brandywine Hundred area.

It is located near Concord Pike (Route 202), between Fairfax and Concord Manor/McDaniel Heights.

Civic Association

The Blue Rock Manor Civic Association provides resident communication and neighborhood information through newsletters, email updates, and this website, where residents can access newsletter content, resource links and phone numbers, dues information, and association contact information.
